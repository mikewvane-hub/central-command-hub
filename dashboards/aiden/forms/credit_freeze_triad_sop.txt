WHY FREEZE AN INFANT'S CREDIT FILE?
Children are 51 times more likely to be victims of synthetic identity theft than adults.
Identity thieves pair a newborn's clean, unmonitored SSN with a fake birth date and address
to apply for credit cards, mortgages, auto loans, and government benefits. A protected
child security freeze creates and immediately locks an empty credit file until the child turns 18.
FREEZES ARE 100% FREE BY FEDERAL LAW.
