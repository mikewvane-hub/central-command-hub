================================================================================
U.S. PASSPORT PHOTO COMPLIANCE GUIDE: NEWBORN & INFANT PROTOCOL
U.S. Department of State Guidelines for Children Under 1 Year
================================================================================

OFFICIAL EXAMPLES & COMPLIANCE URL:
https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/passports/how-apply/photos.html

INFANT PHOTO EXECUTION PROTOCOL:
1. BACKDROP:
   - Lay the baby flat on their back on a clean, plain white or off-white sheet or blanket.
   - Smooth out all wrinkles, folds, and texture.
   - Alternatively, place baby in a car seat covered with a plain white sheet.
   - Ensure ZERO shadows falling across the face or background.

2. LIGHTING & CAMERA ANGLE:
   - Shoot directly from above, with camera parallel to infant's face.
   - Use soft, diffused natural daylight (e.g. near a large window, avoiding harsh direct sun).
   - Ensure even illumination across both sides of the face with no flash hot-spots.

3. FACIAL EXPRESSION & EYES:
   - Eyes must be open and looking toward the camera lens.
   - Department of State concession for infants: If baby's eyes are partially open or not looking
     directly at camera, it is acceptable, but full eye-contact is preferred.
   - Neutral expression or gentle mouth relaxation.

4. CRITICAL ZERO-DEFECT RULES (AUTOMATIC REJECTION CRITERIA):
   [X] NO hands or fingers visible (parents cannot hold infant's head or cheeks).
   [X] NO pacifiers, bottles, or toys in the frame.
   [X] NO hats, bonnets, headbands, or bows covering the hairline.
   [X] NO colored clothing that blends into the white backdrop (dress baby in dark/colored onesie).

5. PHYSICAL PRINT SPECIFICATIONS:
   - Exactly 2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm).
   - Head height (from bottom of chin to top of head) must be between 1 inch and 1 3/8 inches (25 - 35 mm).
   - Printed on high-resolution glossy or matte photo paper.
