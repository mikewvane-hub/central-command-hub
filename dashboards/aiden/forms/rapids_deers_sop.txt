ID CARD OFFICE ONLINE (DIGITAL PRE-SIGN):
https://www.dmdc.osd.mil/self_service

PURPOSE:
Enroll infant dependent (Baby Aiden) into the Defense Enrollment Eligibility
Reporting System (DEERS) and establish military medical benefits (TRICARE).

MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR APPOINTMENT:
1. Certified State Birth Certificate (Raised/embossed seal or colored state security paper).
   * Note: Hospital souvenir certificates are NOT accepted by RAPIDS operators.
2. Social Security Card (or official SSA Form SSA-2853 / formal SSA intake receipt if card is in transit).
   * Note: If SSN has not arrived, infant can be enrolled with a temporary 90-day DoD-generated ID number,
     but the physical SSN must be presented within 90 days to prevent automatic termination of benefits.
3. Marriage Certificate (original or certified copy, if not already verified in DEERS).
4. Sponsor and Spouse Two (2) Forms of Unexpired Government ID:
   - Primary: Common Access Card (CAC) or State Driver's License / Real ID.
   - Secondary: U.S. Passport, Social Security Card, or Voter Registration Card.
5. If Sponsor Cannot Attend in Person:
   - Option A: Digitally sign DD Form 1172-2 via CAC on ID Card Office Online within 90 days.
   - Option B: Execute a Special Power of Attorney (POA) granting spouse authority to enroll dependents into DEERS.

RAPIDS SITE CHECKLIST:
- Double check that the infant's full legal name, DOB, and relationship code (Child) match the birth certificate exactly.
- Confirm TRICARE eligibility flag is set to 'ACTIVE' immediately in DEERS.
- Obtain signed copy of the completed DD Form 1172-2 for your personal family records binder.
