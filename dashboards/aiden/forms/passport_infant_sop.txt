APPOINTMENT SCHEDULER:
https://tools.usps.com/rcas.htm
STATE DEPT MINOR PASSPORT REQUIREMENTS:
https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/passports/need-passport/under-16.html

CRITICAL STATUTORY RULES:
1. IN-PERSON APPEARANCE:
   The infant and BOTH parents must appear in person at a designated Passport
   Acceptance Facility (Post Office or County Clerk).
2. IF ONE PARENT CANNOT APPEAR:
   The non-appearing parent must complete, sign, and date a NOTARIZED Form DS-3053
   (Statement of Consent) in front of a licensed notary public, accompanied by
   a clear photocopy of the front and back of their government ID.
   URL: https://eforms.state.gov/Forms/ds3053.pdf

APPLICATION DOSSIER CHECKLIST:
[ ] Form DS-11: Filled out completely online using the Form Filler, printed single-sided.
    DO NOT SIGN until instructed by the passport acceptance agent.
[ ] Evidence of U.S. Citizenship:
    Official Certified Birth Certificate (Must list both parents' names, date/place of birth,
    registrar's signature, and raised/embossed seal).
    * Note: The Department of State will mail the original certificate back separately.
[ ] Parental Relationship Proof:
    Certified Birth Certificate serves as both citizenship proof and parentage proof.
[ ] Parental Identification:
    Both parents must present valid unexpired photo ID (Driver's License / Real ID, Military CAC, or Passport).
    Bring clear photocopies of the front and back of each ID on 8.5x11 inch white paper.
[ ] Passport Photo:
    One 2x2 inch color photo meeting all infant compliance rules (see infant_photo_specs.txt).
[ ] Separate Payments:
    1. Application Fee: Payable to 'U.S. Department of State' via Check or Money Order:
       - Passport Book (Under 16): $100.00
       - Expedited Processing (Optional): +$60.00
       - 1-2 Day Return Delivery (Optional): +$21.36
    2. Execution Fee: Payable to Acceptance Facility (USPS / Clerk) via Card or Cash:
       - Acceptance Fee: $35.00
